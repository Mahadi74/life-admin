# Privacy Policy for Life Admin

**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026

## Introduction

Life Admin ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles your information.

## Information Collection and Use

### What We Collect

Life Admin is designed with privacy in mind and **does not collect or transmit any personal data**.

- **No Account Required**: Life Admin does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.
- **Local Storage Only**: All your reminder data is stored locally on your device using Room Database.
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not sync your data to any cloud service or external servers.
- **No Analytics**: We do not collect usage analytics or tracking data.
- **No Third-Party Services**: We do not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

### What We Store

All data is stored locally on your device:
- **Reminder Information**: Titles, descriptions, due dates, recurrence patterns, amounts, and notes
- **Categories**: Custom categories you create
- **User Preferences**: App settings like theme preference and notification settings

### Data You Control

- **Backup & Restore**: You can manually export your data to a JSON file stored on your device
- **Complete Control**: You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the app
- **No Remote Access**: We have no ability to access, view, or retrieve your data

## Permissions

Life Admin requests the following permissions:

### Required Permissions

1. **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)**
   - **Purpose**: To send you reminder notifications at scheduled times
   - **Usage**: Only used to alert you about your reminders
   - **Required**: Yes, for core app functionality

2. **Alarm & Reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM)**
   - **Purpose**: To schedule precise reminder notifications
   - **Usage**: Ensures notifications fire at exact scheduled times
   - **Required**: Yes, for accurate reminders

3. **Boot Receiver (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)**
   - **Purpose**: To reschedule reminder notifications after device restart
   - **Usage**: Ensures your reminders work even after rebooting
   - **Required**: Yes, to maintain reminder reliability

### No Internet Permission

Life Admin **does not** request internet permission and operates completely offline.

## Data Security

- **Local Encryption**: Data is stored in an encrypted SQLite database using Room
- **No Transmission**: Your data never leaves your device
- **No Backups to Cloud**: App data is only backed up if you manually export it
- **Secure Storage**: We follow Android security best practices for local data storage

## Data Retention

- Data is retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app
- No data is retained on any external servers (we don't have any servers)

## Data Deletion

You can delete your data in the following ways:

1. **Individual Reminders**: Delete reminders from the app
2. **All Data**: Uninstall the app to permanently delete all local data
3. **Cache**: Clear app data from Android Settings > Apps > Life Admin > Storage

## Children's Privacy

Life Admin does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. The app can be safely used by all ages as no data is collected or transmitted.
